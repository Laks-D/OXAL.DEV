The Oxal Process § 01
01
Analyse
We map the molecular structure of your problem — identifying every bond, every dependency, every hidden constraint. No assumption goes unquestioned.
02
Dissolve
Like oxalic acid on rust: we strip complexity to its base elements. What can't be reduced is redesigned. Nothing survives without earning its place.
03
Compose
Microsolutions crystallize. Each one precise, tested, and purposeful. We bind them with the elegance of a periodic table — where every element has a reason.
04
Resolve
The final compound: a unified solution indistinguishable from inevitability. Designed to look like it could not have been any other way.
